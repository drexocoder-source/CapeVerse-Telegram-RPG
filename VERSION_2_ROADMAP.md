# CapeVerse Version 2 Roadmap

Version 2 is a future major update and is not part of the current Version 0.8 implementation.

## Proposed major systems

- Three-character tactical battles with switching, assists, targeting, status effects, and team ultimates
- Alliances with cooperative raid bosses, alliance missions, ranks, and shared progression
- Safe audited trading with confirmations, rarity rules, cooldowns, and anti-duplication checks
- Seasonal story arcs, battle passes, leaderboards, achievements, titles, and limited events
- Full character mastery trees with branching builds and role specialization
- Expanded Research Archives with suit/form collections, lore timelines, blueprints, and research materials
- World map progression across universes, cities, realms, factions, and story chapters
- Owner web dashboard for content review, balance simulation, events, economy, and player moderation
- Advanced AI balance laboratory that simulates battles before publishing new characters
- Cooperative social features such as friends, spectating, replays, alliance chat links, and raid scheduling

## Design rule

Original CapeVerse characters may use evolution and transformation systems. Licensed characters, suits, and documented franchise forms use Research Archives and require verified rights-approved content.
